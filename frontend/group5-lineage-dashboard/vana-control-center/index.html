3. Why it matters
VANA governance policies mandate verified evidence before automated dispatch. To prevent unverified external or uncalibrated readings from authorizing operational actions, the system fails closed to ABSTAIN (action paused).
4. Next operational steps
No automated action is permitted without complete physical evidence. Environmental officers should check the observation source or wait for edge sensor verification. Authorized administrators may inspect configuration or execute replay verification.
